# Product guidelines

Use research-native tables, measures and bindings. Exactly three pages: CHHHS Publications, Monthly Publications, Source Reconciliation. CanonicalKey counting includes DORA-only records; facility counts overlap. Do not sum them into the HHS total.

Do not relabel or merely hide the old employment model. Remove legacy metadata, filters, bookmarks, queries and caches from the release candidate while preserving the original outside the candidate. Do not ban legitimate publication titles that discuss employment or demographics.

Separate citation publication date, RSS timestamp, retrieval timestamp and persisted first-observed timestamp. Never invent January membership from a year-only or uncorroborated RSS date. Use Australia/Brisbane month boundaries.

Full citations must remain accessible and exports must contain every selected canonical record. Keep uncertainty/coverage labels prominent; do not present unresolved duplicate candidates as a verified unique-paper census.

Do not claim live work from local fixtures or static checks. Exact target IDs, version/definition checks, native execution and read-back evidence are required. No autonomous production publication, sharing changes, purchase, flow activation or VM installation.
